## Decision required before claiming milestone progress

The owner should record one explicit product-order decision: whether the
source-verified **2023 comparator remains the first user-facing comparator**
while 2026 ingestion proceeds privately, or whether the 2026 comparator now
takes that place. Record its effective date and exactly which roadmap clauses
it supersedes. Until then, report 2023 P0/P1 and 2026 E1.4-2026 as distinct
tracks; do not infer a release sequence from a build default, a merged
technical PR, or the newer date alone.

Either option keeps the same independent gates: an approved 2023 inclusion
rule and candidature/document manifest before any 2023 completeness claim;
source-specific authenticity and rights decisions; exact-source human
statement and relationship review; reviewed mobile/quiz content; and a
separate public release decision. PR #101 needs conflict resolution and new
combined-tree CI on its own branch, plus private evidence and human review.
Neither option authorizes importing private PDFs into Git or publishing
political answers.

OpenSpec status, route defaults, source registers or editorial records. Once
the owner resolves precedence, a separate reviewed roadmap/OpenSpec change
can align the documents and tests without silently promoting either corpus.
