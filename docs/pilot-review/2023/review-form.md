## Human checks

For each reviewer, retain a dated decision and evidence reference in the approved private store. Bind the decision to this exact candidate revision and source checksum. A completed worksheet does not itself change a publication gate.

- [ ] Source identity, version, election and checksum checked.
- [ ] Physical page, printed label and section inspected in the original.
- [ ] Summary and any translation preserve the source meaning.
- [ ] All material conditions and limits preserved.
- [ ] Neutral wording checked; no inference from silence.
- [ ] Source rights and any quote permission established independently.
- [ ] First human review retained with identity, date and evidence reference.
- [ ] Second distinct human review retained; secondary reviewer is not the drafter.
- [ ] Open objections resolved in a new candidate revision where needed.

## Comparison review

Complete separately after both source statements pass review. Identify both statement IDs and revisions, the precise comparable instrument, relation type and grounds. Record differences in eligibility, scope, funding, timing and competence. Obtain authentic relationship review evidence. Leave the relationship absent or invalidated if comparability cannot be established.

## Publication handoff

- [ ] Authentic reviews entered through the editorial workflow; no fixture or automated review marks.
- [ ] Rights and programme verification gates passed through the authorized workflow.
- [ ] Source-linked static/browser parity and pending gaps checked.
- [ ] Release acceptance recorded in issue #44 before hosted delivery in #71.

Until these gates pass, keep each candidate unpublished. Do not use this worksheet to infer a programme's lack of a proposal or to authorize a release.
