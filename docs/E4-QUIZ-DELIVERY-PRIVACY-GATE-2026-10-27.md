## Decision needed before G5

Task 4.3 remains **open**. The delivery/privacy owners must first choose and
review an operational policy that meets the quiz spec's zero retained
visitor-level records, or keep the quiz unavailable on that delivery path.
Short retention or pseudonymization alone is not the stated contract.
Do not turn off security diagnostics by inference from this audit: record what
each system stores, distinguish transient connection processing from retained
records, and preserve identifier-free aggregate health and incident signals.

A reviewable evidence packet should contain, without raw visitor data:

1. Effective container, host-proxy and logging-driver configuration; the field
   inventory and retention policy for warning/error events and journals.
2. Cloudflare zone/rule export for every intended quiz hostname, including RUM,
   analytics, WAF/request logs, cookies, cache and error products; provider
   processing/retention decision and owner sign-off.
3. Monitoring and alert-sink inventory with the same field/retention check, plus
   a synthetic, fictional-answer browser/network trace from the exact candidate
   artifact at 360/390/430 CSS px.
4. A recheck after any public-hostname, provider, image or observability change.
   Keep build hash, config revision, observer, date and unresolved findings in the
   G5 record; never retain actual political answers in that packet.
